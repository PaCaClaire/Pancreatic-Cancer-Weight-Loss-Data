SEARCH STRINGS
==============

Systematic review: Weight loss prior to pancreatic cancer diagnosis
PROSPERO registration: CRD42022302985

Search platform: PubMed (Advanced Search), and equivalent platforms for
Web of Science, Embase, Scopus and Cochrane Library (see field mapping
below).

The same five-line concept structure was used across all five databases.
Concepts were combined with AND; terms within each concept were combined
with OR.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
CORE SEARCH STRATEGY (as run in PubMed)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. (Pancreas OR Pancreatic) ab.ti
2. (Ductal Adenocarcinoma OR PDAC OR Cancer OR Malignancy OR Adenocarcinoma
    OR Tumour OR Tumor) ab.ti
3. (Weight OR BMI OR Body Mass Index) ab.ti
4. (loss OR decrease OR change) ab.ti
5. diagnos* ab.ti

Combined as: 1 AND 2 AND 3 AND 4 AND 5

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIELD MAPPING BY DATABASE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The same search string (above) was applied in every database. Only the
title/abstract field code differs by platform:

Database                         Field searched
----------------------------------------------------------------
PubMed                           Abstract/Title (ab.ti)
Web of Science                   Topic field
Embase                           Title/Abstract/Author Keywords field
Scopus                           Title/Abstract/Keywords field
Cochrane Library                 Title/Abstract/Keyword field

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

- No date, language, or publication-type filters were applied at the
  search stage.
- Searches were conducted up to March 2025.
- Full screening results (titles/abstracts and full texts) are
  summarised in the PRISMA flow diagram (Figure 1 of the manuscript).
